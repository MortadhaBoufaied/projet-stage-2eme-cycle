# Requirements Traceability

- Two financial AI agents: implemented.
- Payment behavior and history: implemented with leakage-safe historical aggregates.
- Risk probability, categories, drivers, and mitigation guidance: implemented.
- Revenue, cashflow, and demand targets: supported through the administrator-selected target contract.
- Future forecast horizon: implemented for 1 to 365 days.
- Abnormal-pattern and decline detection: implemented.
- Financial and stock alerts: implemented in the result interface.
- Human-readable summaries and recommendations: displayed after analyses.
- Explainability: local model sensitivity plus versioned column semantics.
- Interactive demonstration: Streamlit role-based UI.
- Evaluated models: validation and untouched test metrics.
- Technical and experimental documentation: included in `docs/`.
